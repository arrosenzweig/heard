Why Heard

"People need to be heard for their true strifes and get real advice from a biblical rather than worldly perspective."

  • From experience: "Especially in the MBA there is a lot of temptation to follow the majority opinion or cross boundaries that had previously been firm for myself."
  • For: anyone struggling with bad decisions, decisions yet to make, or decisions they have no power in.
  • The gap: "There isn't an adaptable app for each person's own journey and values."

Alexandria Rosenzweig, in her own words

What is new

  • Your tradition, not a generic one. Pick one or more of 14, including "exploring."
  • "Did I hear you right?" Say a belief in your own words; Heard restates it and waits for your yes.
  • The whole Bible, your canon, verified. 97,993 verses, three translations; Catholics get the deuterocanon.
  • Sponsor-style matching. People who came through your struggle rank first.
  • An accountability agent. Check-ins on your schedule; it names the trend and brings in people when you slide.

The agents

Two model calls per response. Retrieval, verification and matching are code: zero tokens, fully auditable.

Impact

Sponsorship in recovery: having a sponsor predicted greater early abstinence (Tonigan & Rice, 2010; one study, from summaries).

Honest status: 0 real users so far. All building and testing activity is marked as test data and excluded.

Next: a small consenting pilot with a church or campus ministry, measuring commitments held over weeks.

Limits & next steps

  • Measure the Claude path (written, not yet run).
  • Bigger safety set, reviewed by crisis-line-trained people; oblique language.
  • Semantic retrieval: lexical search can match words but miss meaning.
  • Real moderation for person-to-person contact.
  • Reminders that reach people (check-ins are in-app only today).
  • Crisis resources beyond the US.
